## record: 2026-06-11 · seven-runes
**title:** Seven Runes of Clarity: Hold Your Agency When Working with AI Agents

tags: `#human-ai-collab` `#ai-agency` `#seven-runes` `#human-agent-delegation` `#h-zone-routing` `#check-loop-mvp`
vibe: delegation clarity

- **observed:** practitioners delegating tasks to autonomous agentic AI systems face a delegation clarity crisis, lacking lightweight tools to maintain risks, values, and execution ownership.
- **applied:** developed a 7-question reflection framework (PROBLEMS, INTENTS, DELIVERABLES, PLAN, CHECK-LOOP-MVP, REPORT, NEXT) incorporating explicit Human/Agent/Code (H/A/C) routing and H-Zone mandatory judgment gates.
- **concluded:** explicit H/A/C routing and mandatory human judgment gates protect value alignment, eliminate task evasiveness, and preserve human agency across autonomous agent workflows.
